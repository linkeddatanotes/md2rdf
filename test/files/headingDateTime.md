## 19:25 Adoption of the Paris Agreement

At the end of the conference, after Laurent Fabius banged the gavel to declare the Paris Agreement under the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change adopted, applause broke loose. 

Fabius, French President François Hollande, UN Secretary-General Ban Ki-moon stood up.

Christiana Figueres needed a minute to let it sink it. It had been 20 years since she started negotiating for climate justice in 1995 for Costa Rica and 5 years of negotiations since she had been appointed Executive Secretary of the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change in July 2010. A lifetime of her work concluded. Christiana Figureres stood up last. 

Together, they raise their hands. 

An iconic photo of the Adoption of the Paris Agreement is taken.
